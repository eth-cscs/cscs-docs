# Changing your account data

You can update some of your account data yourself in the user portal at [user-account.cscs.ch](https://user-account.cscs.ch), under **Account**.
Depending on the data, a change is either applied immediately or reviewed by CSCS before it is applied.

| Data | What happens |
|------|--------------|
| Email address | Reviewed by CSCS, then confirmed by you from the new address |
| Affiliation (organisation) | Reviewed by CSCS |
| Primary project (default Unix group) | Applied immediately |

Your primary project can be any open project you are a member of.
It is the default Unix group of your account on CSCS systems, for example for files you create.

To change any other account data, contact CSCS via the [Service Desk](https://support.cscs.ch).

!!! note
    Only personal user accounts can change their data here.
    Service accounts are managed by their project, see [requesting a service account][ref-account-create-service-account].

[](){#ref-account-change-request}
## Requesting a change

On the **Account** page, click **Request account data change**.
The form shows your current data: change what you need and, if useful, add a comment for the reviewer.

* A new primary project is saved as soon as you submit the form.
* A new email address or affiliation is sent to CSCS for review.
* You can change both kinds of data in a single submission: the primary project is applied, the rest is reviewed.

You receive an email when your request is approved or rejected, and a notice whenever a change is applied to your account.

!!! info "Changing your email address"
    After CSCS approves a new email address, a confirmation link is sent to the **new** address.
    Your email address only changes once you open that link and confirm.
    The link is valid for 7 days: if it expires, submit a new request.

!!! info "One open request at a time"
    You can have one open request at a time.
    While it is waiting for review, you can still edit it or withdraw it.

[](){#ref-account-change-follow}
## Following your requests

Use **My change requests** on the **Account** page to see your requests.
It shows your open request, which you can edit or withdraw (**Withdraw request**) while it is pending, and all your past requests and changes.
Each entry shows what was changed, your comment, and the reason or note from CSCS when a request was rejected or approved.

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Pending review | Waiting for CSCS to review it |
| Awaiting email confirmation | Approved; open the confirmation link sent to your new email address |
| Completed | Approved and applied |
| Applied directly | Applied immediately, no review needed (primary project) |
| Rejected | Not applied; the reason from CSCS is shown with the request |
| Cancelled | Withdrawn by you |
| Expired | The email confirmation link was not used within 7 days |
