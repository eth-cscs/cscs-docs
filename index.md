# CSCS Documentation

The Alps Research Infrastructure hosts multiple platforms and clusters targeting different communities. A good spot to get started is with the documentation for the platform that your project is running on.

- **Platforms**

  Projects at CSCS are granted access to [clusters](<https://docs.cscs.ch/clusters/index.md#alps-clusters> "Alps Clusters"), which are managed by platforms.

  [HPC Platform (**Daint**, **Eiger**)](<https://docs.cscs.ch/platforms/hpcp/index.md#hpc-platform>)

  [Machine Learning Platform (**Clariden**, **Bristen**)](<https://docs.cscs.ch/platforms/mlp/index.md#machine-learning-platform> "Machine learning platform")

  [Climate and Weather Platform (**Santis**)](<https://docs.cscs.ch/platforms/cwp/index.md#climate-and-weather-platform>)

  For an overview of the different platforms:

  [Platforms overview](<https://docs.cscs.ch/alps/platforms/index.md#platforms-on-alps> "Platforms on Alps")

Alps is a general-purpose compute and data Research Infrastructure (RI) open to the broad community of researchers in Switzerland and the rest of the world. Find out more about Alps…
